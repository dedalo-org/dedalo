# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want Dedalo to be a project where contributing is judged on the work, and
where the people doing that work are treated decently. Everyone participating
— contributors, maintainers, reviewers, people filing issues — is expected to
help keep it that way.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are about context, not motive.
- Critique the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that reviewers may say no, and that maintainers may say "not yet".
- Respect that people contribute on their own time and in their own language.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, deliberate intimidation,
sustained disruption, publishing others' private information, or any conduct a
reasonable person would find hostile in a professional setting.

Because this project handles money, one thing more: attempting to manipulate
attribution, inflate contribution scores, or claim payouts for work that is not
yours is a conduct violation, not just a technical one.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/dedalo-org/dedalo/security/advisories/new)
if the matter is sensitive, or by opening an issue if it is not. Maintainers
will respond as promptly as they can, keep reports confidential where possible,
and may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour
warrants it.

Maintainers who do not uphold this document in good faith may themselves face
consequences decided by the rest of the maintainer group.
